# Étkezés modul – források

## Elsődleges evidencia

- WHO: *Guideline for complementary feeding of infants and young children 6–23 months of age* (2023)
- WHO: *Infant and young child feeding* fact sheet
- WHO: *Safety and effectiveness of responsive feeding* – systematic review and meta-analysis
- WHO systematic reviews: unhealthy foods and beverages; animal-source foods; fruits, vegetables, nuts, pulses and seeds
- ESPGHAN Committee on Nutrition: *Complementary Feeding – A Position Paper* (2017)
- CDC: *Choking Hazards*
- NIAID: peanut allergy prevention addendum guideline
- LEAP randomized trial
- BLISS development paper and randomized trial
- Dallacker et al.: family meals meta-analysis
- Taylor & Emmett: picky eating review
